File 01 // The Island Reacts

Detection. Escalation. Termination.

Nothing on the island is passive. Enemies respond to noise, combat, and visibility — and every sortie you make feeds their awareness.

Step 01

Detection

Being spotted raises local alert levels. Sound carries. Shadows are borrowed time.

Step 02

Escalation

High attention triggers a rapid enemy response. The island shifts from patrol to pursuit.

Step 03

Terminate

Fail to disengage, and heavy reinforcements are deployed. At that point, the smartest move is knowing when to run.

Sometimes the smartest move isn't to win the fight — it's knowing when to run. Infiltrate outposts, unlock powerups, and escape alive before the island turns against you.

File 03 // Hostile Environments

3 Biomes. 3 Threats.

Procedural seeds, weather cycles, time-of-day. The island never looks the same twice.

Biome 01

Snowy Mountains

Cold, exposed, vertical. Sniper lines and falling hazards. Sound carries further here — every shot is a broadcast.

Biome 02

Forest

Dense cover, ambush opportunities, limited sightlines. Stealth thrives — but so do surprises.

Biome 03

Beach / Underwater

Coastal patrols and submerged complexes. Pressure, darkness, sonar ghosts. Sound is your only map.
